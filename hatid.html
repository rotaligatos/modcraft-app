      Users

      Who can use HATID, their role, and which company they see
      Manage in Command Center ↗
      Users are added, changed and switched off in the Command Center (HATID users tab), by a Modcraft Admin or Director. Changes show here right away. This list is read-only. It also covers password resets and signing steps.
      Roles
      • Admin — everything, including this user list. Always sees all companies.
      • Manager — everything except this user list, including cost reports.
      • Dispatcher — jobs, scheduling, fleet, zones & rates. No Reports tab or cost totals.
      • Field crew — the phone view: their day's jobs, status updates, photos, signatures, expenses.
      • Office viewer — sees and signs delivery schedules only (e.g. the admin office supervisor). Can't change jobs.
      Logins are shared with other apps on this system (e.g. Social Content Manager). Switching someone off in the Command Center only removes their HATID access.
