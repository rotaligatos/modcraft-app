HATID — Privacy Policy

HATID (Haul and Trip Information & Dispatch) · World Class Laminate, Inc. and its sister companies Module System and Services, Inc. and Cebu World Laminate, Inc. · Last updated 22 November 2026

What HATID is

HATID is an internal logistics app used by our staff and drivers to schedule deliveries and client pick-ups, track trips and confirm deliveries. It is not a public service: only people our administrators have registered can sign in.

Signing in with Google

When you choose "Sign in with Google", Google tells HATID your name and email address. HATID uses them only to check that your account is registered and to show who did what in the app. HATID does not ask Google for, read or store your contacts, files, mail, calendar or any other Google data, and it never posts anything on your behalf.

Information HATID keeps

  • Your name, email, role and company, as set up by our administrators.
  • The work you record: delivery and pick-up status, timestamps, notes, photos taken for proof of delivery or loading, and signatures you save.
  • For drivers, the phone's location while a trip is running, used to record departure, arrival and progress. It is not collected outside a trip.
  • Client delivery details needed to do the job (client name, address, contact number, items).

How it is used and shared

This information is used only to run deliveries and pick-ups, notify the people involved (the client, the sales agent and our staff) and prepare internal reports. It is not sold and not shared with advertisers. It is stored with our service providers (Supabase for the database and Google Workspace for files and messages), who process it on our behalf.

Keeping and removing data

Records are kept as long as they are needed for our business and legal requirements. To ask what we hold about you, correct it or have your access removed, contact us at the address below.
